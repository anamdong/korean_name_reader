# Japanese kana pronunciation variants

The stop-coda nasalization rule was checked against Japanese Wikipedia
notation-variant records retrieved on 2026-08-04. Those records include:

1. `ユン・ソンニョル`
2. `ユン・ソンヨル`
3. `ユン・ソギョル`
4. `ユン・ソクヨル`

The app applies the corresponding variants to Korean name syllable boundaries
with a `ㄱ`, `ㄷ`, `ㅂ`, `ㅇ`, or `ㅁ` coda before `ㄹ`, or before a duum-law
glide. The nasalized forms are ranked above literal spellings, while all forms
remain searchable.

The same source set also includes both the conventional `ユ・ソンリョン` and
the phonetic `ユ・ソンニョン` for a Korean historical figure.
