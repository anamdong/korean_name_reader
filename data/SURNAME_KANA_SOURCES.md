# Japanese surname kana priors

The preferred Japanese-kana reading for every surname in `name_index.json` is
drawn from the local-reading column in Japanese Wikipedia's
[`朝鮮人の姓の一覧`](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9C%9D%E9%AE%AE%E4%BA%BA%E3%81%AE%E5%A7%93%E3%81%AE%E4%B8%80%E8%A6%A7), retrieved on 2026-08-04.

This table is based on South Korean surname data and gives the Japanese
reading for each Korean surname. The app retains separately attested Japanese
full-name spellings as lower-ranked aliases where they differ from the table.
